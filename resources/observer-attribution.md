- Eoghan Henn / rebelytics, [One Skill to Rule Them All](https://github.com/rebelytics/one-skill-to-rule-them-all/tree/49d3c488af6311963e18e1a4fd9c309ff3c61b80), source revision `49d3c488af6311963e18e1a4fd9c309ff3c61b80`.
